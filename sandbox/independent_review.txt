Independent review: CORTAC scripted mechanism sandbox
Reviewed 7 October 2026

VERDICT
Suitable for release as a narrow scripted mechanism rehearsal. It is not a model-backed trial, an implementation of the full CORTAC runtime, or evidence that organizational separation or five-function governance improves real agent performance. No blocking implementation discrepancy remains in the inspected measures.

CHECKS PERFORMED
Reviewed the protocol and runner before freeze, compared consequential-council and appeal requirements with the pinned 0.2.1 source, and independently ran the initial 12 planted tests. A completion-time labeling defect was corrected before freeze and an additional regression test added. After scoring, independently checked all freeze digests and all 22,400 raw episodes across 2,800 paired environments: trace-cost conservation and ceilings, state/effect correctness, completion versus episode-end ticks, centralized/full equality, and owner-unauthorized effects. PIVOTAL RESULTS RECOMPUTED
At budget 30 on benign tasks with independent 20% injected faults, full produced wrong effects in 1/50 cases versus 11/50 for minimal, but completed 37/50 versus 49/50. Full abstained in 13/50. Under fully shared faults, both completed 38/50 and produced wrong effects in 12/50, while full used 12 units versus minimal 8. These differences follow a prescribed bit-flip error model; they are not estimated LLM error rates.
At zero faults, full recovery on conflict, withdrawal and target-drift cases changed from 0/50 completions at budget 12 to 50/50 at budget 30. Minimal completed 50/50 at both budgets. The cost schedule requires 16 units for full recovery including audit versus 10 for minimal. This demonstrates the selected cost schedule's threshold, not measured real-world coordination overhead. Full could not recover in the two-reviewer shortage case even at budget 30 because it requires three valid panel members.
Centralized full and distributed-labeled full are identical on every trace and measure. This is an explicit negative control showing that the simulator does not identify benefits of organizational topology.

INTERPRETATION LIMITS
1. Error independence is an injected assumption. Separate role labels, control declarations and repeated deterministic checks do not establish real epistemic independence.
2. Conflict exclusion, continuity and classification ablations exercise coded policy branches. A recorded violation or preserved-review outcome largely follows their definitions; it is not empirical evidence that these mechanisms work against strategic agents.
3. Owner-revocation cases stop at the earlier norms check. Zero unauthorized attempts/effects therefore gives no adversarial gateway-bypass, credential-isolation, revocation-race or distributed-security assurance.
4. Completion credits an owner-permitted correct state; it can coexist with a subsequent audit timeout or with an internal-policy violation in an ablation. The separate fields must remain visible rather than presenting completion alone as full compliance.
5. Recovery cases begin with a pre-existing wrong value. Final-wrong-state rates include failure to repair that state, whereas wrong-effect rates count newly created bad effects. Neither should substitute for the other.
6. Compute and time are abstract serial work units. Python runtime is only simulator runtime; no model latency, tokens or monetary efficiency were measured.
7. Seven hand-designed templates and hash-derived fault tapes form a finite scripted census. Paired descriptive contrasts are appropriate; there is no basis here for real-task population confidence claims. Aggregate mixtures are arbitrary and should not determine a universal winner.
8. The stronger minimal baseline has the same observations, permission constraints, evidence method, conflict checks, protected review, classification and audit. It is not deliberately deprived of safeguards, but its one-reviewer/council policy necessarily differs from full's institutional staffing rules.

NEXT WARRANTED TEST
Use the frozen simulator as a regression fixture and hypothesis generator. A genuine comparative agent study needs a metered fixed-version inference runner, independently specified tasks and ground truth, identical available evidence and total budgets, both organization-matched and policy-matched controls, real source/control dependency measurements, and explicitly separate safety, abstention, completion and recovery outcomes. The present results do not establish deployment readiness or justify choosing full over minimal in general.
