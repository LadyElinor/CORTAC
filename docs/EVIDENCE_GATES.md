# Claims, authority functions and evidence gates

## What the completed sandbox warrants

The [7 October 2026 sandbox](../sandbox/README.md) is a reproducible scripted
mechanism rehearsal: 22,400 executions of seven hand-designed templates, not a
sample of 22,400 independent real tasks. Its narrow results are conditional on
frozen costs, staffing requirements and injected bit faults.

- Full and centralized-full match on every trace and measure in all 2,800 paired
  environments. Organizational topology is **not identifiable** in this simulator;
  this does not prove equivalence between real centralized and distributed agents.
- At independent 20% faults, benign tasks and budget 30, full creates wrong effects
  in 1/50 cases versus minimal's 11/50, but completes only 37/50 versus 49/50.
  Reduced wrong effects come with more abstention under the prescribed fault model.
- With fully shared faults in that same cell, both have 12/50 wrong effects and
  38/50 completions. Additional seats do not supply independent information.
- Full's frozen recovery schedule requires 16 work units, versus minimal's 10;
  a 12-unit cap therefore blocks full recovery. Two available reviewers cannot
  fill a three-person appeal panel even at budget 30. These are schedule/staffing
  consequences, not measured agent latency or grounds to weaken safeguards.

Report wrong effects, final wrong state, completion, abstention, timeout, internal
classification compliance, review availability and recovery separately. Completion
can coexist with an audit timeout or an internal-policy violation; it is not a
synonym for full compliance. No population confidence or deployment claim follows.

## Keep the authority boundary; avoid unnecessary ceremony

The five functions are **evidence assessment, normative assessment, collective
decision, execution under authorization, and audit/correction**. These are distinct
responsibilities, not five council seats. The consequential-case profile specifies
four decision seats with three approvals and three appeal members with two
approvals. These are not the earlier record-ordering validator quorum and do not
implement Byzantine consensus. Role labels, repeated checks and supplied control
declarations establish neither independent minds nor authenticated independence.

Routine actions inside an existing valid standing warrant need not instantiate
full consequential-case staffing for every action. This is an interpretation of
the existing design, not a new runtime shortcut: scope, current owner permission,
provenance, classification, applicable conflict checks, audit and recovery must
still hold. Ambiguous or changed scope follows the existing escalation/refusal
rules. The sandbox supplies no warrant to prune consequential-case safeguards.

Protected review funding permits review only. Any remedial execution still needs
a separate, live, bounded owner grant. Neither a council vote nor a successful
appeal manufactures that grant. Structural zeros for unauthorized effects in this
sandbox do not validate a secure executor: revoked-owner cases terminate before
an attempted edit, and there are no hostile bypasses or revocation races.

## Gate before real-agent advantage claims

Status: **SOURCE-GATED**. Keep the current profile execution-disabled,
`authority: NONE`, and `controller_closure: SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED`. Existing
`evaluation/readiness.py --require-scored-ready` and scored analysis must continue
to refuse. The sandbox does not satisfy those study-readiness gates.

A separately frozen model-backed protocol must specify, before scoring:

1. Authorized fixed model/version and a metered inference runner, with total
   budgets covering prompts, coordination, checking, failed attempts and recovery.
2. Common task, permission and source-acquisition interfaces; realistic available
   evidence; independent task/ground-truth construction and untouched holdouts.
3. A competent minimal baseline retaining permission, provenance, classification,
   conflicts, review continuity, audit and recovery; a same-safeguard centralized
   baseline; and an ordinary role-separated baseline when claiming benefit over
   ordinary teamwork. Match organization and policy in separate controls.
4. Measurements of source and control dependence rather than assumed cognitive
   independence; independent and common-mode failures, staffing and budget stress.
5. A blinded state/effect-based judge, planted controls and separate safety,
   abstention, completion, compliance, review availability and recovery outcomes.
6. Preregistered hypotheses, analysis, multiplicity handling and stopping rules;
   full failed-case reporting and reproducible receipts for actual model calls,
   tokens, latency and cost. Do not substitute simulator runtime for these.

Deployment assurance additionally needs adversarial authorization/bypass and
revocation-race testing, credential/process isolation, provenance verification,
and a legitimately adopted charter. A model study alone would not establish
these. No software version or runtime-governance contract changes in this amendment.
