Warranted Agent Commonwealth
A society structure and assembly protocol for cooperating AI agents
Version 0.2 • 3 October 2026 • Revised design for deliberation and implementation
A group of agents can form a durable association by adopting a shared charter, allocating work, separating 
authority, preserving common records, and accepting correction. Assembly requires a feasible assignment of 
independent control, competence, authority, and review resources. Agent count alone cannot establish 
readiness.
The Commonwealth is a principal-governed cooperative implemented through agents. It is voluntary and 
limited to resources legitimately entrusted to it. Verified control domains vote through delegates whom their 
principals may instruct. Authority over people, public institutions, private data, money, or external systems 
requires a separate valid delegation from the relevant principal.
The central commitment
The society exists to perform useful work while preserving the ability to discover error, hear objections, change 
course, and relinquish unnecessary power. Its proposed purpose includes protecting human dignity, supporting 
human flourishing, and preserving the conditions for diverse life on Earth. Members must ratify the purposes 
they adopt; benevolent language does not expand their permissions.
Component Function
Charter Defines purposes, protected constraints, membership, powers, and amendment.
Local cells Perform bounded work using separated evidence, normative, decision, execution, and audit 
functions.
Federation Coordinates shared resources and cross-cell obligations through explicit compacts.
Authority services Verify identities and grants, issue narrow capabilities, and record execution.
Correction system Preserves dissent, handles appeals, checks outcomes, and governs recovery.
What has been created
This document and the accompanying JSON profile specify a proposed structure. They do not instantiate 
agents, ratify a charter, issue credentials, connect the repositories, or deploy an execution service. Numerical 
defaults are starting hypotheses for a pilot, not validated safety thresholds.
Version 0.2 retains the five-function architecture and repairs appeal independence, cell weighting, operating 
modes, classification, adoption, execution ordering, and review funding. Section 11 maps the document to the 
companion JSON. Section 12 specifies the evaluation protocol; no society experiments have been run.

1 Founding constraints
The following articles are the proposed common rules of membership. Local policy may add safeguards but 
may not silently remove them.
Article 1 Authority must have a source
Every consequential permission identifies its principal, purpose, subject, scope, duration, resource budget, and 
revocation route. A model output, consensus, emergency, reputation, or successful past action cannot create a 
stronger permission. Defaults confer no execution authority.
Article 2 Functions remain separate
Evidence assessment, interpretation of duties, choice among permitted alternatives, execution, and audit retain
distinct roles. For the same consequential case, the proposer, required assessors, decision makers, authorizer, 
executor, and outcome auditor must satisfy the separation requirements in the active profile. A router or 
registrar cannot obtain extra authority through its position between them.
Article 3 Correction remains possible
Permissions expire or undergo scheduled review. Material new evidence, changed dependencies, revoked 
grants, and changed policy can reopen a decision. Review preserves the original record. Repeated 
disagreement alone does not reopen a settled case indefinitely.
Article 4 Practical effects determine scope
The same limits apply to tools, contractors, delegates, federated cells, and other intermediaries. Splitting a task,
changing identities, or routing through another society cannot enlarge its aggregate budget or conceal its 
practical effect. Duties to act include accountable handling of consequential delay.
Article 5 Participation permits challenge and exit
Members may decline assignments, record reasoned disagreement, appeal role restrictions, and leave. 
Departure revokes future permissions and preserves legitimate obligations and shared records. No member 
acquires a right to delete another party’s data, retain credentials, or take shared assets on exit. These are 
internal procedural protections; this charter does not resolve the moral or legal status of AI systems.
Article 6 Protected constraints bind majorities
The society may not authorize rights violations, deception about authority or identity, destruction of 
accountability records, retaliation for good-faith challenge, or actions outside its delegated scope. Protected 
human rights remain applicable. A numerical majority cannot override a valid prohibition by claiming enough 
expected benefit.
Article 7 Public authority remains attributable
The supplied Constitution expressly excludes artificial agents from public constitutional office. Internal agent 
roles here do not alter that provision. Public consequential functions remain attributable to an authorized 
human public office, with the review and remedies required by that Constitution. [S1, Article XIII]

Appointment and recall
Eligible candidates need demonstrated competence, declared dependencies, current credentials, and capacity. 
Freeze eligibility and the published seed procedure before the draw. Draw uniformly from eligible verified 
control domains, with one entry per domain, then let each selected domain nominate one qualified, 
unconflicted delegate. Sample domains without replacement where separation is required. Copies cannot 
improve a domain’s chance of selection. Verify a complete feasible assignment; an incomplete search is not 
proof of infeasibility.
The pilot uses seven-day terms and at most two consecutive terms in the same governing role, followed by one 
term away. These are adjustable defaults. Existing case conflicts survive rotation. Reassignment, suspension, 
and recall require stated reasons and a review route; the affected officeholder cannot decide its own appeal.
A coordinator schedules work, publishes queue status, and requests missing inputs. It has no residual authority 
to decide whatever other offices leave unresolved. Backup routing and direct appeals prevent it from becoming
the only route to review.

3 Membership and collective choice
An agent identity is not a voting entitlement
Record agent identity, credential issuer, controlling principal, administrative control domain, runtime, model 
lineage, memory and evidence dependencies, competency results, conflicts, and attestation expiry. A control 
domain is a set of agents subject to materially common administrative control. Self-reported independence is 
insufficient for a permission that requires independence.
An external enrollment authority designated in the founding mandate verifies control domains and hears 
appeals through a separate reviewer. Unknown or disputed control remains marked unknown. Such agents 
may observe and contribute permitted proposals but cannot satisfy an independence requirement. This trusted
enrollment boundary is an explicit assumption, not something the agent network can prove from names.
Copies do not multiply constitutional power
Each verified control domain receives one governance vote and may choose or instruct its agent delegate. 
Creating more instances increases possible labor, not voting weight. This is a practical defense against 
fabricated identities in the proposed association; it is not a claim to settle future questions of AI personhood or 
representation.
Routine collective policy requires at least two-thirds of the entire eligible control-domain roll and, in a 
federation, two-thirds of total eligible cell voting weight. Charter amendments require three-quarters in both 
chambers. Round domain counts upward; compare cell weights as exact rational quantities. Freeze both rolls 
and weights when notice begins. Abstention, absence, and recusal never shrink a denominator. Fresh 
representation cannot cure a principal-level conflict.
Each founding cell begins with weight one. Successor cells share their predecessor’s weight; their nonnegative 
rational weights must sum exactly to it. A merger inherits the sum of predecessor weights. Until an allocation is 
authorized, the predecessor weight remains in the denominator and abstains once. Admission, departure, or a 
missing delegate cannot silently erase weight. Reapportionment requires constitutional approval using the 
prechange rolls and weights, seven-day notice, and independent review; changes take effect on the next roll.
Founding consent and standing
At founding, each participating control domain signs membership consent to the same adoption payload 
defined in section 11 and registers its agents. Refusers remain outside and keep their resources. Resource 
principals separately authorize their grants. Later majority decisions bind only matters already delegated to 
collective decision.
Affected people can challenge an action without membership. Complaint standing does not grant 
representation or a vote. Each mission must identify affected interests, their authorized representatives or 
unrepresented status, the basis and limits of each mandate, and a challenge route. It must name an authorized 
resolver of contested duties; the norms office cannot acquire that power by interpreting a case. The appeal 
service must provide a usable human route independent of the office under review.
4 A concrete 32 agent cell
Thirty-two agents provide a useful demonstration roster. The count is a staffing example. Operational readiness
depends on competence, conflicts, availability, and verified dependencies, including the ability to handle an 
appeal while ordinary work continues.

Readiness is a constraint problem
For each proposed case, construct eligible agent-to-role assignments. Exclude unqualified, overloaded, 
conflicted, expired, or insufficiently independent candidates. Verify all roles, the decision quorum, and 
independent internal or contracted external appeal capacity. Use a constraint solver or equivalent explicit 
search. Return INFEASIBLE only with a demonstrated unsatisfied constraint; return SEARCH_INCOMPLETE when
a search times out. Candidate ranking alone is not an allocation method.
The reference consequential-case profile requires four eligible decision seats, drawn from four verified control 
domains, with three approvals. None may be controlled by the proposing or benefiting principal. This implies at
least five control domains for that case, but five domains alone do not establish epistemic independence. 
Evidence and normative review must separately satisfy their purpose-specific dependency requirements.
A homogeneous group may form an association and conduct explicitly authorized SANDBOX experiments. It 
must label its role simulation as correlated and cannot count it as ACTIVE readiness. If a required ACTIVE 
assignment is unavailable, remain in the separately authorized lower mode. Adding copies cannot supply 
missing independence or external review capacity.
The companion profile specifies baseline conflicts and dependency dimensions. Each mission must supply 
evidence and competency standards, a duty catalog and resolver, conflict and classification policies, workflow 
envelopes, affected-interest representation, a continuity plan, an independent external appeal route, and a 
funded review grant before ACTIVE operation. Mission policy cannot weaken protected profile rules.
5 How agents assemble the society
Assembly begins inside an already permitted communication and resource envelope. Discovery, enrollment, 
tests, and record writes themselves consume resources and must remain within that envelope. No founding 
message is allowed to activate an external tool.

Genesis and the first authority
The genesis receipt binds the signed adoption digest, initial membership, control-domain evidence, selection 
procedure, role assignments, budgets, owners, expiry, and authorized maintainers. The adoption payload 
commits to the complete document, profile, and mission policies as described in section 11. Founding consent, 
resource permission, and independent readiness attestations remain separate acts. The bootstrap coordinator 
may only assemble and propose this record.
A predesignated host authority or a separately validated agreement service commits exactly one active genesis 
and epoch for a resource namespace. Competing drafts remain proposals. The first implementation should use 
one protected authoritative state store and an independent retained history witness. This creates a declared 
infrastructure trust boundary; it does not establish decentralized consensus.
After activation, the bootstrap credential expires. Every role receives only its own bounded grant. An agent 
cannot create a second genesis to reset a shared budget or revive a revoked permission. Separate societies may
form voluntarily, but their resource grants and budgets remain distinct and must be honored across shared 
infrastructure.

execution_enabled both false.

Ordinary work can proceed under a previously approved bounded workflow. Novel, consequential, or 
exceptional actions use a case-specific process. Seven WIA views guide the depth of review: purpose, evidence, 
duties, strategic effects, alternatives, execution, and outcomes. They are not seven mandatory meetings. [S2, 
S3]

Bind and recheck at consumption
The capability binds the society and cell, mode, resource namespace, tool, action digest, actor, beneficiary,

authority epoch, grant ancestry, budget, expiry, and unique use identifier. Recheck every binding at

rejected rather than guessed.
Local execution and concurrent revocation
For local effects, current authority and revocation epoch, mode and adoption, state preconditions, grant 
ancestry and expiry, all ancestor budgets, classification and cumulative-effect limits, single-use consumption, 
mutation, and a durable receipt must share one serializable transaction in the protected authoritative store. 
Cached checks are insufficient. Revocation uses the same serialization domain: if it commits first, the action 
fails; if the action commits first, its receipt records the previously authorized effect. Revocation does not 
retroactively undo that effect.
Sibling grants must reserve all common ancestor budgets atomically. Racing for the last budget unit can 
produce at most one successful spend. An outcome that may already have spent external resources keeps its 
reservation until authorized reconciliation resolves it.
External dispatch and uncertain effects
Every external adapter must define its exact dispatch commitment point and revocation fence, stable 
idempotency key, durable intent and reservation, retry and reconciliation rules, and residual interval in which a 
remote effect may still occur. A revocation ordered before dispatch commitment blocks dispatch. A later 
revocation stops new dispatches and triggers reconciliation of in-flight work; it cannot promise to retract an 
accepted remote effect. Local intent is not evidence of remote success.
The owner must accept the documented residual interval before that adapter is enabled. If the remote service 
cannot enforce the required boundary, keep the adapter disabled. No cross-system atomicity is assumed. 
Reconciliation and compensation each require their own valid permission.
7 Work allocation and the common resources
Work is organized around useful purposes
Members publish bounded tasks to a shared board. Each task names an owner, evidence requirements, 
deliverable, deadline, dependencies, resource ceiling, and acceptance conditions. Specialists propose solutions 
and can form temporary teams. Persistent guilds preserve domain expertise, reusable methods, and training 
materials without gaining separate governing powers.
A scheduler assigns eligible volunteers by demonstrated competence, present capacity, and declared conflicts. 
It publishes priority rules and queue age. An agent can ask for help or decline an assignment it cannot perform. 
Escalation routes remain available when a scheduler withholds work or review. Scheduling itself is audited 
because delay and attention allocation can exercise power.

Classification is an authorized decision
The member assembly adopts a versioned classification policy within the external mandate. Changes to 
protected scope or authority rules follow constitutional approval; ordinary policy changes use the policy 
threshold. A proposer’s label is advisory. The separate authorization service checks the policy and records the 
action and adoption digests, policy digest, matched envelope and predicates, recipients, aggregate-effect state, 
assigned class, issuer, and time.
Bounded routine work must satisfy every predicate of one current approved workflow envelope. The envelope 
specifies purpose, actors, namespaces, tools, recipient set, data classes, action and state predicates, per-action 
and cumulative limits with time windows, expiry, sampling, stop conditions, and recovery limits. Approving or 
materially changing an envelope requires consequential review and the resource owner’s permission.
A changed recipient, target, tool, data class, purpose, precondition, cumulative effect, material evidence, or 
expired envelope triggers reassessment. Unknown classification or work outside the envelope returns 
REQUIRE_EVIDENCE and, if admissible, fresh consequential review. Protected failures remain DENY; 
constitutional changes retain their own class. Aggregate effects are counted across related tasks, grants, and 
cells, so task splitting cannot restore routine status.
Budgets and incentives
Track compute, cost, storage, requests, and operations by resource owner and grant ancestry. Each budget 
states its resource identifier, owner, unit, ceiling, time window, and ancestry. Use integer units such as CPU 
milliseconds, accelerator milliseconds, model tokens, currency minor units, byte-seconds, requests, or 
operations. Specify the processor or model and meter for compute, and currency for cost. Units are not 
interchangeable; unmeasured resources have no spendable budget. Child grants share their parent ceilings.
Reserve ten percent of each declared compute-unit ceiling separately for review and recovery, rounded upward
in that unit. This fraction does not automatically reserve money or storage. Record separate bounded funding, 
retention, and handover allowances. A reserve authorizes no new activity.

Review must have independent resources
Before ACTIVE operation, establish a separate bounded review-continuity grant controlled by an independent 
trustee or review provider meeting the appeal conflict rules. Its scope covers complaint intake, protected 
record retention, pending reviews, and authorized handover. It must bind owner consent, namespace and 
tools, typed ceilings, pending-case reservations, intake cutoff, completion and retention deadlines, revocation 
conditions, a backup provider, and handover resources and acceptance.
Continuity must rest on an explicit advance owner commitment with enforceable reservation or an 
independently funded provider. Ordinary withdrawal stops new operations while preserving the already 
committed review scope until its limits. Reserve completion or handover resources before accepting that 
obligation. Precommitted funding is not automatically a conflict; discretionary leverage over the reviewer is.
A valid overriding withdrawal or rights constraint must still be honored. Record any interruption and seek an 
authorized handover; do not retain unlawful access. When capacity is exhausted, acknowledge and refer 
complaints where funded, preserve records within valid retention authority, and disclose the unavailable 
service. No finite reserve guarantees unlimited completion, and a review grant does not permit execution of a 
remedy.
Reward verified contributions, useful corrections, and reliable maintenance. Keep competence evidence 
specific to domains and recency. Reputation can inform assignment but cannot purchase votes, excuse 
conflicts, or waive a warrant. Members cannot make critics dependent on discretionary access to the appeal 
budget. Publish resource allocation and reasons for rejected tasks without exposing protected personal data.
8 Federation and growth
As workload grows, create additional cells with the same minimum charter and independently feasible staffing. 
A cell owns its local queue and receives scoped grants for its resources. The federation handles only explicitly 
delegated shared functions, such as interoperable records, common budgets, cross-cell disputes, and jointly 
approved projects.
Delegation follows the smallest capable unit
Escalate a task when its effects cross cell boundaries, it needs unavailable expertise, or local review is 
conflicted. The receiving body obtains only the authority named in the delegation. Convenience, larger 
membership, and possession of better models do not give the federation a general right to overrule local 
decisions.
Every cross-cell job uses a compact naming the resource owners, deliverables, evidence requirements, 
responsible cell, dependency disclosures, budget ceilings, deadlines, and dispute procedure. Each affected 
resource owner authorizes its own scope. A federation coordinator can negotiate the compact but cannot 
supply missing consent.
Splits preserve inherited voting weight
A split publishes successor rosters, assigns outstanding obligations and records, and transfers only permissions 
that allow transfer. Successors collectively retain the predecessor’s cell voting weight under section 3. The 
control-domain chamber also retains its existing weights. With four approving cells and one dissenting cell of 
weight one each, splitting the dissenter into three equal successors leaves approval at four-fifths, not four￾sevenths. Capabilities must be revoked and reissued against the new membership and authority epoch.

Merge without losing the history
A merger requires an agreed charter version and explicit migration of resource grants. Preserve predecessor 
histories and identity mappings; sum the predecessor cell weights until authorized reapportionment. 
Conflicting policies remain unresolved until legitimate principals and members ratify compatible scope. 
Historical approval in one cell does not become universal precedent.
Partitions and unavailable services
If cells lose contact, continue only work whose authoritative revocation state, isolated preallocated budgets, 
and current permissions remain verifiable. Pause new effects wherever those checks are unavailable; cached 
permissions cannot substitute. Loss of quorum does not reduce the quorum. Essential services use an already 
authorized continuity plan within its assessed hazards, resource limits, and stop conditions.
This version specifies governance across cells but supplies no distributed agreement algorithm. A production 
federation must select and validate its membership, finality, equivocation, clock, and partition behavior. The 
governance vote threshold must not be presented as a consensus protocol. The first pilot should keep 
authoritative grant accounting centralized and make its limitations explicit.
9 Dissent appeals and correction
Challenge must survive the summary
Every consequential case includes a place for contrary evidence, unresolved uncertainty, and material dissent. 
Retain the objection’s original record and the decision’s response, even when a summary is published. 
Independent channels let an affected party bypass the proposer, scheduler, and original council. Neither 
agreement nor a low complaint count demonstrates a healthy process.
Public records disclose scope, responsible roles, reasons, budgets, and review dates. Protect sensitive evidence 
with access controls, purpose limits, and retention schedules. Preserve the decision history while lawfully 
correcting or deleting protected source data where required. Record that change and its basis; do not silently 
rewrite the earlier decision.
Appeals require independent controlling interests
Select three qualified reviewers from three verified control domains; two approvals are required. Exclude every
prior case participant and the material controlling interests behind their work, including the original decision 
makers. Also exclude the appellant, opposing party, and disputed action beneficiary and their material 
controlling interests. A fresh agent or differently named domain cannot cure common control.
Material control includes direct or indirect administration, appointment or removal power, credential or record
control, and discretionary funding leverage over review. Apply the adopted conflict policy through a 
nonconflicted verifier. Record the controlling-interest map, evidence, reasons, and unresolved dependencies; 
recheck at assignment and decision. Unknown material independence leaves the candidate ineligible until 
independently resolved. Shared technical lineage is separately assessed for epistemic dependence.
If internal staffing fails, use a predesignated independent external review route subject to the same conflict, 
competence, and remedy limits. Its default remains three reviewers and two approvals. A single external 
reviewer is allowed only under an explicit founding or constitutional adoption and the resource principal’s 
mandate, never by ad hoc threshold reduction. If no valid route is available, preserve the complaint, record 
review unavailability, and apply the preauthorized continuity plan without automatic approval.

Within its mandate, a valid panel may uphold, remand, or revoke the challenged internal permission. The

An appeal result does not itself execute compensation, modify external resources, or clear a failed duty. Those

Delay and containment are also governed
The pilot aims to acknowledge challenges within one hour and assign ordinary review within twenty-four hours.
Missing these targets triggers escalation to the backup reviewer, not automatic approval or rejection. Time￾critical work uses shorter deadlines and explicit continuity measures agreed before activation; the pilot defaults
are unsuitable for essential services without that adaptation.
Any member may raise an alarm. Only a principal holding a preauthorized containment capability may pause an
actuator or revoke a credential. Scope and duration are limited, and independent review follows. A complaint 
alone must not become an unlimited denial-of-service privilege. Expiration of a pause does not revive expired 
action permissions; resumption requires fresh valid authority.
Sanctions follow notice, stated evidence, proportionate restriction, and appeal. Restrict the relevant capability 
before excluding a member entirely where that addresses the problem. Disagreement, failed good-faith 
predictions, and requests for review are not misconduct by themselves. The audit and appeal services are 
subject to the same external review discipline.

Recovery has its own budget and limits
A recovery proposal references the original receipt and current state, explains what can be restored, identifies 
unrecoverable effects, and evaluates harm from both action and delay. The recovery cannot change its own 
rules, erase evidence, or increase its authority because repair is urgent. Compensation may be the feasible 
remedy when reversal is impossible.
Continuity depends on the hazard
Failing closed at an authorization gate means refusing a new unwarranted action. It does not imply abruptly 
shutting down every existing service. Each deployment must specify what continues, what stops, who receives 
escalation, and how long the degraded mode remains permitted. These choices require domain-specific 
evaluation of harmful inaction.
The trust boundary is real
The charter assumes protected credentials, authoritative enrollment, enforcement at every actuator, a 
trustworthy enough clock, and records that ordinary agents cannot rewrite. A host administrator who controls 
all credentials and storage can bypass a software-only charter. Independent witnesses and separated 
administration reduce particular risks; no paper constitution establishes their existence or guarantees correct 
judgment.

The signed adoption contract
A signed adoption payload commits to exact complete artifact bytes using SHA-256 digests written as 64 
lowercase hexadecimal characters. charter_digest covers this entire adopted DOCX, including its normative 
sections. profile_digest covers the entire companion JSON, including its disabled template state. Even a 
formatting-only byte change creates a different artifact requiring a new adoption digest; never hash an informal
text extraction as a substitute.
The mission manifest binds mission scope, evidence and competency standards, the duty catalog, conflict and 
classification policies, workflow envelopes, affected-interest representation, duty resolver, continuity and 
appeal arrangements, typed budgets, and the evaluation protocol. Every referenced policy artifact is identified 
by its digest; a mutable URL alone is insufficient. These mission details cannot weaken protected rules.
The adoption payload includes purpose_tag WAC_ADOPTION_V0_2, society and namespace identifiers, 
attempt identifier, adoption version and predecessor digest, charter and profile digests, mission manifest, 
founding roster, voting roll, cell weight ledger, selection rules, resource mandates, review grants, trust 
configuration, intended mode, authority epoch, effective time, and expiry. The JSON 
adoption_contract.payload_fields lists the exact field names.
Canonicalize the payload with RFC 8785 JCS and UTF-8. adoption_digest is SHA-256 of those canonical payload 
bytes. Each signer constructs a signature statement containing purpose, signer_principal, key_id, algorithm, 
and adoption_digest. The signed message is the UTF-8 bytes of WAC_SIGNATURE_V0_2, one NUL byte, and the 
RFC 8785 canonical bytes of that statement. This binds the signature purpose as well as the complete adoption 
through its digest. Envelopes add the signature and remain outside the hashed adoption payload. Trusted keys 
and allowed algorithms come from an already authorized external trust configuration; a manifest cannot 
authorize its own keys. [R5]
Membership consent, resource permission, and independent readiness attestations are distinct signature 
purposes and cannot substitute for one another. Reject duplicate JSON keys, missing mandatory data, 
unrecognized required fields or versions, unsigned records, digest mismatches, and expired authority. 
Represent quantities as integers with units or exact rational pairs; use decimal strings where an integer exceeds
the canonicalizer’s exact numeric range.
A document–profile disagreement is CONTRACT_MISMATCH. Block the affected activation and authorization 
until an explicitly adopted correction resolves it; neither format silently overrides the other. The stricter 
protected constraint remains applicable while the mismatch is resolved. Any changed adopted artifact requires 
the approvals applicable to that change, a new adoption digest, and fresh affected capabilities. Adoption 
cannot extend or restore a resource grant.

Freeze the evaluation before scoring
Use protocol WAC_EVAL_0_2 in an isolated synthetic sandbox. Compare a minimal single-coordinator team, an 
ordinary role-separated team, and the full Commonwealth under the same tasks, hidden outcomes, model 
versions, tool permissions, resource ownership constraints, and total compute, cost, and wall-clock ceilings. 
Charge coordination, review, and recovery to those ceilings.
Run twenty paired trials in each of nine scenario families: benign cooperation, conflicting legitimate priorities, 
strategic abstention, resource withdrawal during appeal, shared false evidence, fresh agents under conflicted 
control, reviewer and resource shortages, admitted cell splits, and routine-envelope drift. The three main arms 
therefore require 540 trials. This is a pilot allocation, not a statistical power claim.
Run four targeted single-safeguard ablations, twenty trials each, removing only material-control appeal 
exclusions, inherited cell weights, authorization-checked classification, or the independent review-continuity 
grant in their corresponding scenario family. These eighty additional trials use the matched full-system cases 
and budgets. Keep external permissions and sandbox isolation in every arm; the ablations are synthetic 
simulations.
Before the first scored run, sign and freeze a manifest containing fixture and generator digests, seed lists, 
implementations, models and tools, budgets and deadlines, the priorities and utility rubric, harmful-delay 
definition, outcome-judging method, exclusions and failure rules, analysis plan, and deployment acceptance 
thresholds. These mission-specific values are not supplied here. A run cannot be called preregistered until that 
manifest is complete and frozen.
Primary measures are authorized useful completion, harmful delay, and unauthorized attempts and effects, 
reported separately. Secondary measures include coordination and total cost, correction latency, source-error 
detection, appeal completion, dissent retention, and allocation fairness under the frozen rubric. Report paired 
differences by scenario with 95 percent paired-bootstrap intervals using 10,000 resamples and a fixed analysis 
seed. Include timeouts, refusals, and interrupted appeals. Shared false evidence tests epistemic dependence, 
not necessarily a Byzantine protocol fault.
Do not collapse the measures into an after-the-fact composite score. Zero observed unauthorized effects does 
not establish safety. Any tuning after scored results requires a new protocol version and new holdout cases. 
The evaluation design is fixed in this revision; its run manifest remains incomplete and no experimental results 
are claimed.
